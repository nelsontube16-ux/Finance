10 Knowledge Drop — For Your Internship Brain Today's concept · 3 min

How a rate hike actually moves a bank's valuation

When Bank Indonesia hikes 50 bp like today, the market reaction is almost never linear. Three things change at the same time, and a junior who can articulate all three in an interview stands out:

1. Net Interest Margin (NIM). Banks reprice loans faster than deposits (asset-sensitive book), so NIMs widen short-term. This is why BBCA & BBRI typically catch a bid on hike day even if the index sells off.

2. Cost of Risk (CoR). Higher rates eventually compress borrower cashflow → NPL formation rises with a 2–4 quarter lag. The "good" P&L now creates a "bad" provision charge later. This is where the analyst alpha sits: model the lag.

3. Discount rate / multiple. Higher risk-free → higher cost of equity (Ke = Rf + β·ERP). Even with better near-term earnings, P/B and P/E should mechanically compress. The stock can rally on EPS and still be derating — both can be true.

Net: trade is usually long the bank for 1–2 quarters of NIM, then rotate before the CoR cycle prints. That's the difference between a quarterly trader and a structural investor — and it's exactly the kind of nuance MDs want to hear in a stock pitch.

11 Daily Read — One Book, Used Properly IB · PE · M&A
Investment Banking
Rosenbaum & Pearl

Investment Banking: Valuation, LBOs, M&A, and IPOs — Joshua Rosenbaum & Joshua Pearl

This is the technical bible used inside every bulge-bracket analyst training programme. It is not a "read cover-to-cover" book — it's a 20–30 minutes / day reference. The discipline is what compounds.

How to actually use it:

  • Mon–Tue: Trading Comps & Precedent Transactions chapters — re-do one screen end-to-end on a name in the news (today: a US utility for the NextEra/Dominion read-across).
  • Wed–Thu: DCF — re-build one model from a 10-K. Force yourself to defend every WACC input out loud.
  • Fri: LBO & M&A — work through one accretion/dilution and one paper-LBO. This is the single most-tested skill in PE interviews.
