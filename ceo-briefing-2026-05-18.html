Critical

The Strait of Hormuz remains under dual blockade — the central obstacle in all negotiations. Crude flows through the Strait plunged from ~20M bpd pre-war to ~2M bpd. Brent at $110.74, WTI at $105.42. IEA projects 3.9M bpd global supply deficit for 2026. Even immediate resolution wouldn't normalize markets before October. BBC, FT, and Bruegel now project a European energy crisis comparable to 2022. Sulfur and fertilizer supply chains also disrupted — downstream food inflation risk is rising.

🇨🇳 Trump-Xi Beijing Summit: Style Over Substance NEW

The two-day summit (May 13-14) covered trade, Taiwan, Iran, AI, and rare earths. Outcome: disappointing. Analysts expect stabilization, not revitalization. Markets sold off on the anticlimactic result.

McKinsey's 2026 update confirms geopolitical distance is now the single biggest force reshaping global trade geometry, not comparative advantage.

💵 Rupiah Under Pressure: 17,505

The Rupiah hit Rp 17,505 ahead of the MSCI review — its weakest level of the year. The double hit of MSCI exclusions + oil import costs (Indonesia is a net oil importer) creates a self-reinforcing weakness: foreign outflows weaken the currency, which raises import costs, which worsens the current account, which triggers more outflows. Bank Indonesia may need to intervene with reserves or raise rates — both costly.
