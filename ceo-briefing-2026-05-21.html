CRITICAL: Hormuz Crisis Escalated — Missile Strikes on Shipping, Tanker Seizures

Since our April 26 briefing, the Strait of Hormuz crisis has intensified materially. On May 5, the CMA CGM San Antonio was struck by a cruise missile (8 crew injured). On May 7, the Chinese tanker JV Innovation was attacked — the first Chinese vessel targeted, risking Beijing's patience. On May 8, Iran seized the oil tanker Ocean Koi. Iran established a new "Persian Gulf Strait Authority" to regulate all maritime transit. The UK has deployed warships, drones, and fighter aircraft. The IEA calls this "the largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market." Oil at ~$105–110. Action: Reassess all shipping, energy, and EM exposure immediately. The conflict is widening, not stabilizing.

POSITIVE SIGNAL: Trump-Xi Beijing Summit — US-China Trade Détente

The May 14–18 Trump-Xi summit in Beijing produced a meaningful (if fragile) de-escalation: tariff cuts on $30B+ of goods on each side, a new "Board of Trade" and "Board of Investment," 200 Boeing aircraft order, $17B/yr agricultural purchases, rare earth export restrictions lifted. September follow-up summit scheduled in the US. Net: Short-term positive for markets, but structural decoupling continues.

Global Macroeconomics

High Impact

🏢 Fed: Historic 4-Member Dissent — Powell Exit NEW

The April 29 FOMC held rates at 3.50–3.75% with an 8–4 vote — the first 4-member dissent since October 1992. Miran wanted a 25bp cut. Hammack, Kashkari, and Logan opposed the easing bias in the statement. Powell's chairmanship ended May 15. Markets now pricing a 74.5% probability of NO rate changes through end-2026. The probability of a hike has risen to 14.9% (from 0.8% a month ago). The Fed is paralyzed between slowing growth and energy-driven inflation.

📈 Eurozone: Inflation Surging, ECB Frozen

Eurozone headline inflation jumped to 3.0% in April (from 2.6% in March, 1.9% in February) — driven entirely by energy costs. The ECB is holding rates unchanged after cutting four times in H1 2025.
