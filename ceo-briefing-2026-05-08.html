Geopolitical Tensions

Critical • Shifting

⚡ US-Iran Peace Negotiations New

The US sent a 14-point MOU to Iran via Pakistan to formally end the war and reopen Hormuz. Trump paused the "Project Freedom" military escort operation, saying there is "great progress." Oil fell 7.8% on May 6 — the biggest single-day drop since the war began. However, Tehran has not accepted yet. The strait remains effectively closed with only ~20 vessels/day transiting (down from 129 pre-war). IEA calls it "the largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market."

Peace Talks Oil Below $100 Not Confirmed

🇪🇦 UAE Exits OPEC New

UAE left OPEC on May 1, citing quota frustrations and Hormuz disruptions to its own exports. UAE has 4.3M bpd capacity but was pumping only 2.37M bpd under OPEC quotas. If Hormuz reopens, UAE could unleash 1.6M bpd of additional supply (~1.5% of global production), creating massive downward oil price pressure. This is a structural change to the oil market — OPEC's pricing power is diminished.

OPEC Fractured Supply Unlock Oil Bearish

🇨🇳 China Invokes Blocking Rules — First Ever Escalated

China deployed its 2021 Blocking Rules for the first time ever, ordering Chinese companies to ignore US sanctions on five oil refineries processing Iranian crude. International banks face an impossible compliance dilemma — they cannot obey both US and Chinese law simultaneously. However, Chinese regulators quietly told banks to pause new loans to the sanctioned refineries — a mixed signal suggesting China is testing boundaries, not yet committing to full defiance.

Unprecedented Banks Trapped Structural Shift

🇺🇸🇨🇳 Trump-Xi Summit May 14-15 New

Trump proposed a "Board of Trade" mechanism for managing US-China trade — signaling the US has effectively given up on changing China's economic model. A summit is set for May 14-15 in Beijing. CEOs from Nvidia, Apple, and Exxon are being invited. Meanwhile, a US court struck down 10% Section 122 tariffs (narrow relief only). Current effective tariff on Chinese goods: 30%. US imports from China at 15-year lows.

Summit May 14 30% Tariff Tariff Ruling
