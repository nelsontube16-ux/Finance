🚨 Kevin Warsh Confirmed as Fed Chair — New Era Begins Breaking

The Senate confirmed Kevin Warsh as the 11th modern Fed Chair on May 13, 2026 in a 54-45 near party-line vote (only Sen. Fetterman crossed). Powell's term as chair expires May 15 but he stays as governor. Warsh has signaled: (1) changes to inflation measurement methodology, (2) reformed Fed communications, and (3) potential 1–2 rate cuts to bring overnight rates toward 3.0–3.25%. This is the most significant monetary policy leadership change in 8 years. Markets will reprice Fed forward guidance.

Game Changer 54-45 Vote Dove Signal

📊 US Inflation Re-Accelerating — CPI 3.8% Worsened

April CPI came in at 3.8% — above the 3.7% consensus and the highest since May 2023. Energy costs from Hormuz disruption are feeding through to headline numbers. This complicates everything: Warsh inherits a Fed that cannot cut aggressively without fueling inflation, but cannot hold without choking growth. The stagflation whisper is getting louder.
