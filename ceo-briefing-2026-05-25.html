🎮 Iran Deal at "Final Stage" — But Don't Celebrate Yet

The US-Iran conflict is now in its third month. After a two-week ceasefire brokered by Pakistan on April 8, both sides have violated the truce. On May 7, the US hit two ships near Hormuz; Iran retaliated against US naval vessels near Chabahar. Despite this, Trump claimed May 23 that a deal is "largely negotiated." Key issues: Hormuz reopening, Iran's nuclear program, sanctions relief, and a long-term peace agreement. Hormuz traffic remains at 5% of pre-conflict levels — 20M bbl/day of global oil flows are still disrupted.

📈 Inflation: Energy-Driven Resurgence

Eurozone headline inflation surged to 3.0% in April (from 1.9% in February) — driven entirely by energy costs from the Hormuz closure. However, core inflation actually fell in April, suggesting the shock is supply-side, not demand-driven. IMF global inflation forecast: 4.4% for 2026. This creates a central bank nightmare: inflation rising while growth slows. Classic stagflation setup.

IHSG DOWN -30% YTD — Worst Drawdown Since 2020

From our Apr 26 briefing: We flagged IHSG at 7,594 and warned about oil import dependency as a headwind. It's now at ~6,085 — a catastrophic 20% decline in just 29 days. Nine consecutive losing sessions. This is no longer a correction. This is a crisis.

📈 IHSG: The Perfect Storm

IHSG hit a 13-month low at ~6,085, down 30% YTD and on a 9-session losing streak. Three simultaneous headwinds: (1) President Prabowo tightened export rules on palm oil, coal, and ferroalloys, mandating shipments through a single state-owned exporter — this spooked foreign investors. (2) Oil import costs from the Hormuz crisis crushing the current account. (3) Persistent capital outflows as global investors flee to US safety.

-30% YTD Policy Shock Capital Flight

💰 Rupiah Under Siege: Emergency Rate Hike

The rupiah weakened to IDR 17,704/USD (-8.97% over 12 months). Bank Indonesia responded with a surprise 50bps rate hike to 5.25% — the first increase since 2022 and larger than expected. The finance ministry is now injecting IDR 2 trillion daily into the bond market to stabilize the currency. The current account deficit widened to its deepest level in 6+ years.

Emergency Hike CA Deficit Widening Daily Interventions

📋 IPO Pipeline: Ambitious Targets vs. Harsh Reality

One was projected for H1 2026, two for H2. However: Given the IHSG -30% YTD and 9-day losing streak, expect significant delays. 💡 Indonesia: Bottom-Fishing or Falling Knife?
