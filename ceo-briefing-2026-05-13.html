💥 US-Iran War: Ceasefire on "Massive Life Support"

President Trump declared the ceasefire is on "massive life support" after rejecting Iran's counter-proposal on May 11. Tehran demands: (1) war reparations, (2) full sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz, (3) end to all sanctions, (4) release of frozen assets. The US demands: (1) 10-year nuclear halt, (2) surrender of 440kg enriched uranium stockpile. The gap is enormous. Both sides exchanged fire in the Strait on May 7. Iran vowed to "never bow."

Bottom line: There is no credible diplomatic path to Hormuz reopening in the near-term. Plan for $100+ oil through at least Q3 2026.

📈 April CPI: 3.8% — Hottest Since May 2023

Released yesterday (May 12). Headline CPI rose 0.6% MoM, pushing annual inflation to 3.8% — up 50bps from March's 3.3%. Core CPI (ex food/energy): 0.4% MoM, 2.8% annual. The energy index surged 3.8% in April alone, with gasoline up 28.4% YoY and fuel oil up 54.3% YoY. Food rose 0.5%. Airline fares up 20.7% YoY. Real average hourly wages fell 0.5% for the month and 0.3% annually.

So what: Energy is driving 40%+ of headline inflation. This is a supply-side shock, not demand-pull — which makes it harder for the Fed to address without crushing growth.
