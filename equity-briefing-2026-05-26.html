Geopolitics
Market-moving · 48h
DE-ESCALATING

US–Iran framework: 2-month ceasefire extension + Hormuz reopening

Secretary Rubio confirmed "progress" toward a deal. The Trump framework would lift the US blockade while Tehran reopens the Strait of Hormuz — through which ~20M bpd (~20% of global petroleum) flows. Markets pricing the upside.

Why it matters: Single biggest oil-bearish catalyst of the quarter. Reverses the Feb–Apr risk premium. Re-rate energy short, transports/airlines long, EM importers (Indonesia, India) relief — though Indonesia has idiosyncratic FX issues.
FRAGILE

Fresh US military operations in southern Iran today

Brent +3.4% intraday to $99.39 as fresh strikes hit ahead of negotiations. Pattern: military pressure ↑ before talks resume — classic leverage play, but a single misstep collapses the deal.

Position: Asymmetric. Long-vol on oil (straddles), keep a small defense long (LMT, RTX, NOC) as ceasefire-fail hedge.
STRUCTURAL

Russia–Ukraine: ongoing energy/sanctions overhang

Headline fatigue, but European gas, Ag (wheat, fertilizer), and uranium flows still distorted. Continues to push EU industrial onshoring narrative.

Theme: European defense capex cycle (Rheinmetall, BAE, Saab, Leonardo) remains intact even if oil cools.
QUIET

China–Taiwan: not in the top-10 risks for 2026

Post Trump–Xi summit, market consensus (Crisis Group, BlackRock GRI) is tensions stay contained through 2026. China stimulus + US tariff calibration is the real story.

Implication: Underweight tail-risk hedges on Taiwan straits; reweight cyclical EM Asia (KOSPI, TWSE) over geopolitical fear discount.

The 4 concepts behind today's headlines

Geopolitical Risk Premium (oil)
The premium price oil carries because of supply-disruption risk, separate from supply/demand fundamentals. When tensions rise, the premium widens (Apr: ~$15–20/bbl). Ceasefires compress it. For pitches: always split oil price into fundamentals + risk premium when modeling.
"Sell the News" reaction
When a stock falls on great news because the news was already priced in. NVDA today: blowout earnings + $80B buyback → stock −1.8%. Lesson: the market doesn't react to absolute results, only to results vs. positioning. Always check options flow + short interest before earnings.
Net Interest Margin (NIM)
Bank's spread = (Interest earned − Interest paid) / Average earning assets. BI's +50bps hike directly expands Indonesian bank NIM in the near term (loans reprice faster than deposits) — but raises credit risk on the back end.
NIM = (Interest Income − Interest Expense) / Avg Earning Assets
Current Account Deficit → FX Pressure
When a country imports more than it exports (+ net income flows), it needs USD to settle the gap. Indonesia's widened Q1 CAD is the root cause forcing BI to hike rates to defend rupiah. The chain: CAD ↑ → FX ↓ → CB hikes → growth ↓ → equity multiples ↓. Always map this chain in EM pitches.
