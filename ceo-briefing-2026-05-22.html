🔁 Hormuz Blockade → Inflation Re-Acceleration → "Great Reversal" in Central Banks → EM Carnage

The chain reaction we predicted in April is now fully playing out. Oil peaked at $144, PPI hit 6.0%, CPI at 3.8% (forecast 6% in Q2). This forced the ECB, BoE, and BI to pivot from cuts to hikes — a historic reversal. BI's emergency 50bps hike is the most visible casualty: the rupiah hit record lows despite intervention. IHSG's 16.8% crash and zero-then-one IPO pipeline prove that EM markets cannot withstand an energy-driven inflation shock of this magnitude. The April prediction of "this chain reverses rapidly if Hormuz reopens" remains the single most important variable.

🔁 SpaceX IPO ($75B) + Nvidia $80B Buyback = Massive Capital Reallocation Event

In June, SpaceX will attempt to raise $75B — the largest IPO in history. Simultaneously, Nvidia is executing an $80B buyback. Combined, these represent $155B in capital market flows that will reshape institutional positioning. SpaceX will pull capital from other tech allocations, while Nvidia's buyback supports AI stock prices. Net effect: rotation out of mid-cap tech into SpaceX + price support for Nvidia/AI sector. Position for this reallocation before June 12.

🔁 Trump-Xi Trade Truce + US-Iran "Encouraging Signs" = Risk-On Setup if Both Resolve

Two of the three major geopolitical risks (US-China trade war, Iran war, Fed transition) are showing signs of de-escalation. The Beijing summit produced tariff cuts on $30B+ goods and a Boeing deal. Rubio signals progress on Iran. If both resolve in Q3: oil drops to $70-80, inflation falls, Fed cuts, EM rebounds, IHSG recovers, IDX IPO pipeline opens. This is the bull case scenario — probability ~25-30%. The downside scenario (Hormuz escalation, $140+ oil, 6% CPI, no cuts) remains ~35% probable.

🔁 KOSPI +8.4% (Samsung) + Nikkei +3.1% ≠ Hang Seng -1.2% = Asia Is Splitting

Asia is no longer a single trade. Japan and Korea are surging on tech/semiconductor tailwinds (Samsung strike averted, Japan exports +14.8%), while China/HK are lagging on weak domestic data. This bifurcation creates a clear allocation signal: overweight Japan/Korea semiconductors, underweight China consumer. Indonesia sits in the middle — connector economy benefits from both, but currency weakness and oil dependency keep it in the penalty box for now.

🔁 30Y Yield at 5.13% (19-Year High) + S&P Forward P/E at 21x = Valuation Collision Course

The bond market and equity market are telling contradictory stories. Bonds are pricing in persistent, hot inflation (5.13% on 30Y is screaming). Equities are pricing in continued AI earnings dominance at a 21x forward P/E (above 19.9x 5-year avg). One of them is wrong. If bonds are right and inflation stays elevated, equities must reprice lower (fair value closer to 18x = 13% downside on S&P). If equities are right and AI growth overwhelms inflation, bonds will rally. This is the single biggest macro positioning question right now.
