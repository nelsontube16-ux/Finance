Regulators will scrutinize studio consolidation, streaming competition, and cable network concentration. If you hold media assets, comparables have been permanently reset.

💊 Sun Pharma-Organon: India Goes Global

India's largest pharma company paying $11.75B all-cash for Organon is the largest biopharma deal of 2026. This makes Sun Pharma a top-25 global pharma company ($12.4B combined revenue) and the world's 7th-largest biosimilar company. This is a structural shift: Indian firms are now global consolidators, not just manufacturers. Shares up 7% (Sun), 17% (Organon). Expected close early 2027.

⚡ Intel-Apple Foundry Deal: The Geopolitical Trade

Apple and Intel reached a preliminary deal for Intel to manufacture future M-class chips for Macs/iPads, potentially starting production in 2027. The US government's 10% stake in Intel and Commerce Secretary Lutnick personally brokered the deal. This is as much about US semiconductor sovereignty as business strategy — reducing Apple's TSMC dependency amid Taiwan risk. Intel surged +14% on May 8, Apple +2%. Combined with Intel's April earnings beat (+23%), INTC has rallied ~40% in two weeks.

The fundamental story is strong even as the stock market digests structural reforms.

GDP 5.61% Beat Estimates Connector Economy

⚖️ Free-Float Revolution: New Rules Live

IDX has implemented new free-float requirements that will reshape the market:
• Market cap < Rp5T: minimum 25% free float
• Rp5T–Rp50T: minimum 20% free float
• > Rp50T: minimum 15% free float
Existing companies below Rp5T must comply by 2029; larger firms by 2027-2028. This is designed to win back MSCI eligibility after the January freeze. Short-term pain (forced selling) but long-term structural positive for institutional investors.

MSCI Target 15-25% Float Structural Positive

📄 IPO Pipeline Doubled: 15 Companies Queued

IDX now has 15 companies in the IPO pipeline as of April 30 (11 large-scale, 4 medium) — nearly double the 8 we reported on April 26. The exchange targets 50 IPOs for 2026 (revised down from 66). At least 6 "lighthouse" large-cap IPOs are planned. 957 companies are currently listed. The pipeline growth signals improving confidence despite IHSG volatility. Sectors: banking, infrastructure, mining, and tourism.
